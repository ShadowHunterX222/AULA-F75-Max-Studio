# AULA F75 Max Studio v0.7.0

## Expanded effects
- Expanded Studio's software-rendered animation catalogue from 52 to 120 effects.
- Added aurora, comet trails, meteor shower, fire/embers, lava lamp, plasma, nebula/galaxy, starfield, confetti, fireworks, scanner, heartbeat, sonar/radar, orbit, helix, tide/ocean, waterfall, ripple fields, geometric patterns, equalizer, moiré, kaleidoscope, vortex, tunnel, light chase, candlelight, snowfall/frost, heatwave, spectrum bounce, and additional reactive patterns.
- Added effect categories for Nature, Cinematic, Geometric, Rhythm and Party.

## Curated colour palettes
- Added 18 multi-stop palettes: Aurora, Arctic Ice, Sakura, Sunset, Deep Ocean, Ember, Cyberpunk, Lavender Dream, Enchanted Forest, Royal Amethyst, Cotton Candy, Toxic Neon, Champagne Gold, Dracula, Nord, Moonlight, Redline and Citrus Pop.
- Retained the individual colour picker and quick colour swatches. Editing the picker or choosing a quick swatch selects Custom colour.
- Palette selection is saved with Studio state and profile exports.

## Preserved behavior
- Existing AULA real-time HID transport, desktop tray driver and global reactive input remain unchanged.
- All 82 LED slots and the Alt_R 95, Fn 96, Ctrl_R 97 and LED98 98 layout are preserved.
- The custom effects remain PC-rendered and use AULA's real-time lighting transport; no QMK effect IDs or firmware writes are sent.
